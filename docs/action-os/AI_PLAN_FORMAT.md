# Plan format (`buildPlan`)

Sections (only non-empty ones appear): Top priorities · Overdue · Deadlines in the next 7 days · Customer, partner and
supplier follow-ups · Decisions and approvals required · Collections and payments · New business opportunities ·
Blockers and risks · Missing information / not verified · Recommended for today · Confirmed progress.

Executive summary: counts only, e.g. "4 open items across 2 apps: 0 critical, 2 high, 2 overdue, 0 decisions and 0
follow-ups."

Each item keeps priority, title, organisation, source app, kind, due date (or "no due date in the source"), value
(omitted for confidential items), why, rule, verification state, status, suggested next step and source link. The plan
stores a snapshot; the plan page also shows each item's status now.

Verification labels: **Confirmed** (from a source record) · **Reported / not verified** · **Missing information** ·
**AI recommendation**. Suggestions are always labelled and never become claimed commitments.

The digest for e-mail/WhatsApp (`digestText`) carries counts and up to three titles only.
