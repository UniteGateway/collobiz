# How the site is organised (24 Nov 2026)

COLLABIZ is organised around **the four reasons people come**. Each has one clear door.

| Path | For | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy from Unite** | Homes, shops, factories, builders | `/businesses`: solar quotes, Unite products, property, courses |
| **Sell Unite products** | Dealers, distributors, stores, franchises | `/partner-with-unite`: matching agent and application |
| **Grow my business** | Any industry, anywhere | `/join`: Unite Industry Club, with the member's own board |
| **Earn as a partner** | Consultants, agents, EPC companies | `/signup`: protected leads, tenders, teams, commissions |

## Public site
- **Header:** five links (Unite businesses, Sell Unite products, Industry Club, Opportunities, How it works), plus Sign in and one **Get started** button that opens `/start`. Previously there were 11 links and three different "join" buttons.
- **`/start`:** the four paths, explained in plain words.
- **Home page:**
  - hero (Get started · Explore Unite businesses)
  - **the four paths**
  - Unite businesses
  - Sell Unite brand products
  - how it works, with a featured opportunity
  - trust
  - "Not sure where to start?"

  The feature list, the four routes for business and the industry lists moved to **How it works** and the footer, so nothing was lost.
- **Footer:** Get started (the four paths) · Explore · Company · Support, including the Terms and legal documents.

## Signed-in members
- **Top of the menu:** Home, My tasks (the action board) and Messages.
- **Open groups:**
  - **Find & win business:** Opportunities, Leads & CRM, Business offers, Tenders
  - **Unite:** Unite businesses, Sell Unite products (dealer dashboard), Industry Club, Unite Solar, Energy, Powertek
  - **Money:** Earnings, Invoices & P&L, Proof
- **Folded groups** (they open when one of their pages is in use):
  - **Work together:** Collab, Teams, Projects, Micro-contracts, Capacity, Trading, Demand pools, Commit, Launch, Invest
  - **Network:** Community, Global, Performance
- Every page that existed before is still reachable; only the order and grouping changed.

Tests: the browser suite "Simple navigation", plus updated home-page checks. The accessibility scan now includes `/start`, `/partner-with-unite`, `/club` and `/how-it-works`.
