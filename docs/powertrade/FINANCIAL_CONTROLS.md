# Financial controls

1. Savings, sales turnover, benefits and cash are separate fields and widgets; sales are labelled "turnover — not profit".
2. Benefit stages: anticipated (never counted) → earned → **verified by Unite finance** → **collected** (with bank/settlement
   reference; partial collections add up; cannot exceed net).
3. Allocation to a power-expense month: only from collected, not-yet-allocated money; **approved by Unite finance**; the
   approval is audited and notified.
4. Commissions come in from the finance ledger by reference (`commission:<id>`), only when earned or later, once.
5. Product-for-Power: supply value, separately agreed benefit and PPA tariff are separate legs; benefit ≤ supply; tax review
   must be cleared and documents accepted before approval; all documents (invoice, settlement authorisation,
   reconciliation) accepted before completion.
6. No netting of invoices between different legal entities, no fund transfers, no automatic electricity credits.
7. Net economic power expense = month's bill (recorded, else estimated from the profile) − approved support. It is an economic
   comparison; the supplier and DISCOM invoices are paid in full.
