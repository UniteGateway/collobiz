# COLLABIZ POWERTRADE — Do Business. Save on Power. Grow Together.

POWERTRADE is a **shared commercial collaboration layer** inside COLLABIZ (COLLAB → Energy & Business Tie-Ups), not a solar
app. It connects what a business *needs* (lower power cost, customers, suppliers, capital) with what it can *contribute*
(products, capacity, services, rooftops, land, distribution) and keeps energy savings, sales, earned benefits and cash
strictly separate.
