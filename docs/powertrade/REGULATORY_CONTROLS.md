# Regulatory controls

- Open-access eligibility, captive ownership and net-metering rules come from the Power Lab rule set with confidence and
  source; unknown state rules produce a warning, never a silent pass.
- Charges come from versioned records with effective dates and provenance; the shipped set is **illustrative** and every
  result says so until verified orders are entered in the Power Lab.
- Captive / group-captive status is never granted from a simple input: equity must be entered, and the annual consumption
  test and SPV legal review are always flagged.
- No offer is executable until a Unite reviewer records approval after technical, commercial, regulatory and tax checks.
- COLLABIZ does not sell or trade electricity; energy matches route to developers and licensed traders.
- Barter (XCHANGE) is flagged as a taxable supply on both sides; Product-for-Power requires tax review.
